7.5. R을 이용한 분석 예제

Exercise: 사람들의 다이어트 수행이 자존감에 긍정적인 영향을 미치는지를 탐색한 가상 데이터를 사용해 예제를 풀어보자. 이 데이터 셋에는 10명의 실험 참가자들의 다이어트 시작 전부터 세 시점(다이어트 시작 전, 다이어트 시행 3개월 후, 다이어트 시행 6개월 후)까지의 자존감 점수가 반복적으로 측정되었다. 실험 참가자의 각 시점에서의 자존감 점수 평균이 통계적으로 유의미한 차이가 있는지를 분석하기 위해 아래와 같은 영가설과 대안가설이 설정되었다.

  • \(H_{0}:\mu_{\text{time1}}=\mu_{\text{time2}}=\mu_{\text{time3}}\)

  • \(H_{A}:\text{at least two} \; \mu \;\text{are different}\)

STEP2. Check the sphericity assumption

#Mauchly's chi square by prof.bk
mauchly.chi = function(k, n, W) {
  d <- 1 - ((2 * ((k - 1)^2)+(k - 1)+2)/(6*(k - 1)*(n - 1)))
  a <- -(n-1)*d*log(W)
  b <- (k*(k - 1)/2) - 1
  names(a)=c("Mauchly.chi^2")
  names(b)=c("df")
  print(c(a, b))
}

An one-way repeated measures ANOVA was conducted to assess the effect of diet duration on participants’ self-esteem scores across three time points (pre-diet, 3 months after diet initiation, and 6 months after diet initiation). The assumptions of normality and sphericity were assessed using the Shapiro-Wilk test and Mauchly’s test (\(\chi^2(2)=4.77, p =.09\)), respectively. The results confirmed that both assumptions were met. Table 1 shows the descriptive statistics of the data.

As the Table 2 indicated, the result demonstrated that there was a statistically significant difference in self-esteem scores across the three time points at 95% confidence level, F(2, 18) = 55.47, p < .001, \(\eta_G^2\) = .83. In other words, approximately 83% of the variability in self-esteem scores can be attributed to the time elapsed since diet initiation. Additionally, a trend analysis indicated that data were well fit by a significant linear trend, \(t(18)=10.46, p<.001\), suggesting that self-esteem scores increased steadily across time.

Post-hoc comparisons using the Bonferroni correction indicated significant increases in self-esteem scores over time. The mean self-esteem score was lowest at the pre-diet stage (M = 3.14, SD = 0.55), increased significantly after 3 months of dieting (M = 4.93, SD = 0.86), and reached its highest average at 6 months (M = 7.64, SD = 1.14). Each of these differences was statistically significant. In summary, participants’ self-esteem scores showed marked improvement over the diet duration, indicating that continued adherence to the diet may have a positive effect on self-esteem over time.
