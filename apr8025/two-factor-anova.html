8.4. Two-way between ANOVA

Exercise: 이 데이터 셋은 비어고글 효과 현상을 검증하기 위해 수행된 실제 실험을 바탕으로 만들어진 가상 데이터로(Field, 2017), 2(Face type) x 3(Alcohol level) 2-요인 완전랜덤화설계 실험을 통해 연구자는 다음과 같은 세 개의 가설을 검증하고자 한다.

(1) 매력도(Face type)의 주효과

(2) 알콜 강도(Alcohol level)의 주효과

(3) 매력도와 알콜 강도의 상호작용효과

A two-way between-subjects ANOVA was conducted to examine the effect of face type and alcohol on attractiveness ratings, and there was no major violation in assumptions. Table 1 shows the descriptive statistics of the data.

As shown in the Table 2, the result indicated that there was a statistically significant effects in both face type and alcohol, and effect size presented a modest level of impact. Specifically, first, a statistically significant main effect of face type was found, \(F(1,42) = 15.58, p < .001, \eta_G^2 = .27, \omega^2 = .16\). The results showed that attractiveness ratings were higher in participants belonging to the “Attractive” group (M=6.33, SD=0.96) compared to the “Unattractive” group (M=5, SD=1.82).

Second, a statistically significant main effect of alcohol was found, \(F (2,42) = 6.04, p < .01, \eta_G^2 = .22, \omega^2 = .11\). Tukey’s test was employed for post-hoc. The results showed that attractiveness ratings was higher in participants belonging to the “7%” group (M=6.38, SD=1.09) compared to the “placebo” group (M=4.94, SD=1.95), \(M_{\text{diff}}=-1.437, t(42)=-3.475, p<.01.\)

In final, there was a statistically significant interaction between the effects of face type and alcohol on attractiveness ratings, \(F (2,42) = 8.51, p < .001, \eta_G^2 = .29, \omega^2 = .17\). Simple effects analyses were performed to further examine this interaction. For unattractive faces, ratings in the “7%” group (\(M = 6.38, SD = 1.09\)) were significantly higher than those in the “placebo” group (\(M = 4.94, SD = 1.95\)), \(M_{\text{diff}}=-3.125, t(42)=-5.342, p<.001.\) However, for attractive faces, alcohol level did not produce any significant differences. In addition, the difference between attractive and unattractive faces, which was significant under the “placebo” (\(M_{\text{diff}}=-2.88, t(42)=-4.914, p < .001\)) and “4%” (\(M_{\text{diff}}=-1.62, t(42)=-2.778, p = .008\)) groups, disappeared under the “7%” group (\(p = .398\)). These results suggest that alcohol increased the perceived attractiveness of unattractive faces, while ratings of attractive faces remained relatively unchanged (see Figure 1).

8.5. Two-way within ANOVA

Exercise: self-esteem2는 4주 다이어트 프로그램에 참가한 12명의 실험 참가자들로부터 측정한 데이터를 담고 있다. 이들 실험 참가자들은 두 개의 실험 조건, 즉 통제 조건(플라시보)과 특별 다이어트 조건에 모두 노출된 후, 3회(실험 시작: T1, 실험 중간: T2, 실험 종료: T3)에 걸쳐 그들의 자존감 점수가 측정되었다. 이 가상 실험은 단기 다이어트 프로그램이 시간이 지날수록 자존감 점수를 크게 높일 수 있는지를 조사하기 위한 것이다.

(1) 다이어트 프로그램의 주효과

(2) 시간의 주효과

(3) 다이어트 프로그램과 시간의 상호작용효과

#Mauchly's chi square by prof.bk
mauchly.chi = function(k, n, W) {
  d <- 1 - ((2 * ((k - 1)^2)+(k - 1)+2)/(6*(k - 1)*(n - 1)))
  a <- -(n-1)*d*log(W)
  b <- (k*(k - 1)/2) - 1
  names(a)=c("Mauchly.chi^2")
  names(b)=c("df")
  print(c(a, b))
}

A two-way within-subjects ANOVA was conducted to examine the effect of time and treatment on self-esteem score. Prior to the main analysis, assumptions were examined. The Shapiro–Wilk tests indicated that the normality assumption held across all cells except for the t₁ condition in the control group (\(p = .02\)). However, visual inspection of Q–Q plots suggested no major deviation from normality; thus, the analysis proceeded as planned. The assumption of sphericity was tested using Mauchly’s test. The result showed that the sphericity assumption was violated for the main effect of time, \(\chi^2(2) = 7.57, p < .05\), but not for the interaction between time and treatment, \(\chi^2(2) = 4.84, p > .05\). Therefore, the Greenhouse–Geisser correction was applied to the main effect of time. Table 1 presents the descriptive statistics of the data.

As shown in the Table 2, the result indicated that there was a statistically significant effects in both time and treatment. Specifically, first, a statistically significant main effect of time was found, \(F(1.31,14.37) = 27.37, p < .001, \eta_G^2 = .05\), indicating that self-esteem scores varied significantly across time points. Second, a statistically significant main effect of treatment was also found, \(F (1,11) = 15.54, p < .001, \eta_G^2 = .06\), showing that self-esteem differed depending on the treatment condition.

In final, there was a statistically significant interaction between the effects of time and treatment on self-esteem score, \(F (2,22) = 30.42, p < .001, \eta_G^2 = .05\). Thus, simple effects analyses were conducted to further examine the nature of this interaction. Within the control condition, self-esteem changed significantly over time, \(F(2,22) = 39.70, p < .001\) (Bonferroni-adjusted), with all pairwise contrasts reaching significance—\(t_1\) vs. \(t_2\): \(t(11)=4.53\), \(t_1\) vs. \(t_3\): \(t(11)=6.91\), and \(t_2\) vs. \(t_3\): \(t(11)=6.49\) (Bonferroni-adjusted \(\text{all} \ ps < .001\)). In contrast, within the Diet condition, the time effect was not significant, \(F(2,22) = 0.078, p = .925\). These results indicate that the observed interaction was driven by significant temporal change in the control group, whereas self-esteem remained stable across time points in the Diet group (see Figure 1).

8.6. Two-way mixed ANOVA

Exercise: LooksOrPersonality 데이터는 10명의 남성과 10명의 여성이 스피드 데이트 이벤트에 참가해 “외모” 또는 “성격”이 다른 9명의 데이트 파트너에 대해 선호도를 평가한 2(성별: 여성, 남성)×3(외모: 매력적인, 평균, 못생긴)×3(카리스마: 높은, 약간, 없는) 3-way mixed 설계로 수집된 가상 데이터 셋이다. 본 예제에서는 포함된 데이터에서 외모에 대한 하위 데이터 셋만을 사용하였다. 성별을 between-subject 요인으로, 외모를 within-subject 요인으로 하는 2(성별: 여성, 남성) × 3(외모: 매력적인, 평균, 못생긴) two-way mixed 설계인 이 가상 실험은 데이트 상대를 평가하는 데 미치는 외모의 영향력이 성별에 따라 어떻게 달라지는지를 조사하기 위한 것이다. 다시 말해, 이성의 평가에 대해 성별과 외모 사이에 유의미한 상호작용이 있는지가 연구자의 주요 관심이다.

(1) 성별의 주효과

A two-way mixed-design ANOVA was conducted to examine the effects of gender (between-subjects factor) and partner attractiveness (within-subjects factor) on participants’ preference ratings. Prior to the main analysis, the assumptions of normality, homogeneity of variance, and sphericity were examined and all were met. Table 1 presents the descriptive statistics of the data.

As shown in Table 2, the results revealed statistically significant main effects of both gender and attractiveness, as well as a significant interaction between the two factors. Specifically, a significant main effect of gender was found, \(F(1,18) = 36.88, p < .001, \eta_G^2 = .49\), indicating that female participants reported higher overall preference ratings than male participants. A significant main effect of partner attractiveness was also observed, \(F(2,36) = 49.29, p < .001, \eta_G^2 = .59\), showing that ratings increased as the partner’s attractiveness level rose. Finally, the interaction between gender and attractiveness was significant, \(F(2,36) = 35.25, p < .001, \eta_G^2 = .51\).

Simple-effects analyses were performed to further examine the interaction between gender and partner attractiveness. … These findings indicate that men’s preference ratings were influenced by partner attractiveness, whereas women’s ratings remained relatively stable regardless of attractiveness level (see Figure 1).
